AI selling is back but yields dropped after weak housing starts: NQ −0.5% pre-open, no clean edge

Monday's AI-led bounce is fading. Nasdaq-100 futures were around 29,536–29,564 at 8:38–8:42 AM ET, down ~0.5–0.6% from Monday's 29,722.5 settle1,7. Markets price an October hike at only ~16–17% but still expect a December hike (Fed in blackout before Oct 27–28)8,9,12. Futures imply an open below Monday's NDX low (29,461). Weak AI sentiment points down, but falling yields push the other way, and Netflix/SpaceX don't report until after the close4. I lean bearish but see no clean edge for the open. Let the first 15–30 minutes decide.

01Why this window
  • The data is out and it softened yields. September housing starts came in at 1.221M (−5.2% m/m) vs ~1.30M consensus, and single-family starts were 859K. Permits were 1.400M vs 1.39M. The 10-year fell to ~5.09% and NQ bounced ~120 points off its 8:30 AM low3,5,7. Pending home sales are due at 10:00 AM ET (consensus ~0.0%)11.
  • AI risk-off is global this morning. The Kospi fell 3.73% and SK Hynix 4.2%. European tech was down 1.4% and ABB fell ~6% despite record orders. In the US premarket, Micron, Sandisk, Intel and AMD were down 2–3%, CoreWeave/Nebius were down ~4–5%, and Apple was down ~1.2%3,4,6.
  • Implied gap below Monday's low. NQ traded ~193 points over cash NDX at Monday's settle (29,722.5 vs 29,529.35). On that basis ~29,550 NQ implies an NDX open near 29,355, just below Monday's 29,461 low and about halfway back down Monday's bounce from 29,1882,7. That's an approximate basis estimate, not a quote.
  • No Fed or megacap catalyst before the close. The Fed is in its quiet period before the Oct 27–28 FOMC. Netflix and SpaceX report after Tuesday's close, and Meta/Tesla report Wednesday. GM's −6–7% premarket drop (on a guidance narrowing) is only a small drag on the Nasdaq3,4,12.
02Opposing case
  • Bull case: Yields are falling, not rising (−5 bp after the soft housing print), the dollar is weaker, and October hike odds are only ~16–17%. Nvidia is barely lower (−0.6%), and futures are already ~120 points off the overnight low. Gap-downs into Monday's range after a strong bounce often get bought, and dip buyers stepped in hard Monday3,6,7,8.
  • Bear case: Monday's bounce looks like a one-day relief rally. Selling is spreading from Asia and Europe into US chips, and CoreWeave's credit (distressed CDS, high Oracle bond yields) is still stressed. Brent at $110–111 keeps the inflation/December-hike story alive. A clean break of the overnight low would likely target Friday's 29,188 NDX close3,4,6,13.
03What would change it
  • Flip to LONG: NDX gets back above Monday's close (29,529; NQ ≈ 29,720) and holds it after 9:30 AM, with the 10-year staying below ~5.10% and chips (SMH) turning green. Stronger: the 10-year moves toward 5.05%.
  • Flip to SHORT: NQ breaks the overnight low (~29,478, ≈ NDX 29,285) and NDX stays below Monday's low (29,461) through the first 30 minutes. Also counts: the 10-year rebounds through ~5.14% or Brent extends above ~$111.5 on Gulf/Houthi headlines.
  • Wildcards: The 10:00 AM pending home sales print, any AI-financing or CoreWeave credit headline, unscheduled Fed/Warsh comments despite the blackout, and Middle East oil headlines3,4,11.
